About Corner Office

Corner Office is a cheerful idle game about climbing the career ladder. You start as an Intern at a small desk on floor 2, tap to work, play quick office minigames and hire funny coworkers until you reach the CEO's office on floor 60.

Taps and combos fill your performance bar for each of the 10 promotions. Three short tasks (Coffee Rush, File Frenzy and Inbox Zero) pay performance and coins, and coworkers like Mia the Designer earn coins every second.

3 quick tips

  1. Tap quickly to build a COMBO, but play the tasks too. One quick minigame, even played badly, can do as much as a dozen taps.
  2. Each task has a cooldown after you play it, so rotate between all three.
  3. In Inbox Zero, avoid the green spam emails from "Spammy" offering FREE PRIZES.
